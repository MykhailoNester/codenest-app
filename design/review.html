the core loop — what the app is for

A task is prioritised → a schedule fires → a session launches in the app with the right agent, project and model → the composer carries context, skills and agents → anything blocking surfaces in Needs You → you intervene in that session → cost lands against the project and task. Everything below is judged against whether it serves this.
